Color Pause - current private development source

This tree contains source and runtime resources, not a bundled Godot executable.
The tested engine is Godot 4.7.2 Standard, official ed1daf0bf. Godot's MIT notice
is in licenses/godot/Godot-LICENSE.txt. An exported distribution must include
the component notices from the exact engine/export template it actually uses.
Such a final APK/AAB/export notice audit is not completed by this source sync.

Fonts: exact Noto Sans Bold, Noto Sans CJK Bold TTC and extracted Noto Sans CJK
SC Bold OTF bytes are retained. Full OFL, copyright and identity/provenance
records are under licenses/fonts/. LXGW 975 Yuan SC 700W is covered by the full
OFL in assets/colorpause_ui_20261030/fonts/LXGW975Yuan-OFL.txt and
assets/font_policy/lxgw-official-ofl.txt. System font fallback is disabled;
explicit current bindings live in scripts/ui_restore/font_policy.gd and
typography.gd. Font licenses do not license unrelated bitmap lettering/art.

Audio: audio/AUDIO_PROVENANCE.json identifies the retained procedural effects
and current Sunlit Steps OGG. The current BGM generator, composition and delivery
contract are retained next to the OGG. The unused old music_loop.wav and preview
OGG are not included. Procedural provenance is not an independent legal opinion
or a new public license grant; device listening balance remains unverified.

Artwork, level data and implementation: retained under the user's private
project authorization. A handoff or this private GitHub upload does not itself
establish public redistribution rights or commercial asset clearance. No new
license grant is made here. Bitmap art-lettering clearance remains distinct
from the audited OFL fonts. See docs/current/STATUS.md for release boundaries.

All included files and exact hashes are listed in docs/current/SYNC_MANIFEST.json.
No real player saves, credentials, signing keys, engine caches or build artifacts
are included. Preserve these notices and licenses with derivative test packages.
