3. Project audio
The seven WAV assets are the project's procedural score and effects produced
by assets/audio/generate_audio.py. Inspection found Python-standard-library
sine-wave/harmonic synthesis, envelopes, and explicit note sequences, with no
external recording or sample input. An isolated rerun reproduced all seven
files byte-for-byte; hashes and synthesis format are in audio/AUDIO_PROVENANCE.json.
This describes observed provenance, not an independent legal originality
opinion or a new open-source license grant. No third-party sound library is
represented as having been licensed.

Preserve THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt, licenses/, and audio/AUDIO_PROVENANCE.json
